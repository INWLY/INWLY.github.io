About

Hi, I am Wentao Ruan, also known as INWLY.

I am a Ph.D. student at the IIP-HCI Lab, Anhui University. My research focuses on brain-computer interfaces and EEG signal processing, mainly exploring decoding in the text space. In my earlier research, I worked on ancient Chinese character image restoration, including detail-aware restoration methods for degraded historical character images.

LWTformer: A Detail-Aware, Learnable Wavelet-Transformer for Ancient Chinese Character Image Restoration

Wentao Ruan, Xinhui Li, Zhan Cheng, Cunhang Fan, Libao Tian, Zhao Lv

LWTformer restores degraded ancient Chinese character images with learnable wavelet downsampling, spatial-enhanced attention, and wavelet-aware refinement for clearer stroke details.
